1. METHODOLOGICAL FOUNDATION:
   - Problem: Hardcoded thresholds (like 0.50 and 0.75) are arbitrary assumptions.
   - Solution: 1D Unsupervised K-Means clustering (K=3) groups workloads into 3 natural
     density tiers based on their objective reclamation composite scores.
   - Decision boundaries are calculated as exact midpoints between adjacent centroids:
       SoftReclaimThreshold = (C0 + C1) / 2
       FullReclaimThreshold = (C1 + C2) / 2
