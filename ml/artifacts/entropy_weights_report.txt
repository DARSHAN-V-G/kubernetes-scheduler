1. METHODOLOGICAL FOUNDATION:
   - Origin: Claude Shannon's Information Entropy (1948) & TOPSIS Decision Science.
   - Principle: Features with higher information dispersion across cluster workloads
     carry greater discriminative contrast and naturally receive higher weights.
   - Eliminates subjective human bias and circular synthetic target formulas.
